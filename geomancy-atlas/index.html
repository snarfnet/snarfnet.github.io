Geomancy Atlas

A bilingual Japanese/English reference and chart-building tool for the sixteen geomantic figures.

Using the app / 使い方

Browse the figures and search Latin, English or Japanese names. In Build, tap the dots of the four Mothers. The chart follows by transposition and parity addition. Select a derived figure to see how it was formed. The optional Reconciler combines the Judge with Mother 1.

「図譜」で名称を調べ、「図表を組む」で母図の点をタップします。生成段階を選び、各図をタップすると計算元を確認できます。「練習」には図形の足し算と名称クイズがあります。

Save named charts with notes in the local journal (up to 500 records). Export the chart as PNG, PDF or SVG. JSON exports include editable notes and can be imported again. Image and PDF exports contain the chart and title, not the full journal notes.

記録は「保存」を押して端末内へ残します。JSONは編集用のバックアップです。PNG・PDF・SVGは図表とタイトルの書き出しで、長文メモは含みません。

Scope & sources / 資料の範囲

This app teaches figure names and chart construction. It does not provide predictions or automatic good/bad judgments. Planetary and house attributions vary by tradition and are not included in this edition.

Historical reference: Cornelius Agrippa, Three Books of Occult Philosophy, Book II, Chapter 48 (English edition, 1651). Figure patterns and parity construction were cross-checked against contemporary reference tables. App explanations are independently written and figures are drawn geometrically. No modern commentary or manuscript scans are reproduced.

Privacy policy / プライバシーポリシー

Geomancy Atlas does not transmit journal entries or usage information to the developer. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking SDKs. Figures and charts are calculated on your device. Records remain in app storage.

When you choose Share, the selected file is passed to the destination you select. External source links open in your browser; those websites have their own privacy policies. Device backups may include app data according to your OS settings.

Delete a record within the app to remove it locally. Deleting the app removes its local storage. Exported files and OS backups must be managed at their respective destinations.

開発者は記録・入力内容・利用状況を収集しません。共有したファイルや外部リンク先の扱いは利用者が選んだサービスに従います。アプリ内の記録は削除できます。OSの設定によって端末バックアップに含まれる場合があります。
